How it works

Four simple steps from enquiry to income.

No jargon, no chasing paperwork. Here's exactly what happens when you work with us.

1

Get in touch

It starts with a conversation. Tell us about your business or project — whether you're an installer doing dozens of jobs a week, a business planning upgrades, or a partner looking to grow. We'll map out which schemes, services and incentives apply to you, and give you a clear picture of the value on the table.

2

We handle the detail

From here, the process is off your plate. We run eligibility checks, conduct audits where needed, gather and verify documentation, and manage compliance across every relevant scheme. This is where claims usually fall over — and where our team earns its keep.

3

Certificates & claims created

With documentation verified, we create your certificates and lodge rebate claims correctly the first time. Real-time compliance checks run throughout, so issues surface instantly rather than weeks later as rejections.

4

You get paid

Certificates are monetised at strong market rates and rebate payments processed fast. You receive clear reporting on every certificate, every claim and every dollar — so you always know exactly what came back and why.

What you don't have to do

You don't need to study scheme rules, chase missing documents, argue with assessors or watch certificate markets. That's our job — and we've built the process so you can focus on yours.

All you need is a project and a phone. Call us on 0415 271 956 and we'll take it from there.
