What we do

Seven connected services that take an energy project from idea to income — with the technology support to keep your business humming.

Certificate Services

We create and monetise energy certificates across federal and state schemes — STCs, VEECs, ESCs and PRCs. Our team manages eligibility, compliance and creation from start to finish, then surrenders or sells the certificates in the way that best suits your financial and environmental goals.

Learn more → STC · VEEC · ESC · PRC

Energy Rebates

Rebate processing built for installers. Lodge claims straight from your phone, get real-time compliance checks as you go, and receive fast payment once approved. If you're claiming for the first time, we walk you through every step — so every eligible installation becomes extra income instead of paperwork.

Learn more → Quick claims · Fast payments

Sales

We provide sales for the installers. Our team sells on your behalf — bundling certificates and rebates into sharper quotes and handling the customer conversations — so you can stay on the tools while the jobs keep coming in.

Learn more → Sales for installers · Quotes

Energy Audits

Our audits pinpoint where your site is wasting energy and money — then map out the upgrades and certificate opportunities that fix it. Choose the audit that suits you: a live on-site audit, a phone audit, or a desktop audit. You get a clear, prioritised report with real numbers, not jargon.

Learn more → Savings · Certificate potential

Aggregation

Pooling certificates across multiple projects and installers means stronger market returns and far simpler administration. We bundle your volumes, manage the trading, and pass the benefits back to you.

Learn more → Better prices · Less admin

IT Support

Managed IT support tailored for trade and energy businesses — devices, email, systems and a responsive helpdesk, including support for the Data Force, Bridgeselect and Alitsy platforms. We keep your technology reliable so you can focus on selling and installing, not troubleshooting.

Learn more → Helpdesk · Devices · Systems

Billing Facility

Billing handled for you. We generate invoices for completed installs and certificate and rebate income, track payments as they land, and manage the billing admin — so your cash flow stays clear and your books stay tidy.

Learn more → Invoices · Payment tracking
